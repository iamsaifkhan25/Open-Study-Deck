Security & Privacy Statement

Open Study Deck offers two ways to generate decks: Guest Access, which routes your request through Open Study Deck's own Cloudflare Function to a shared Gemini credential (no key required, limited daily generations), and Bring Your Own Key (BYOK), which sends requests from your browser directly to Google Gemini using your personal key. In both modes there are no login forms and no subscription plans, and Open Study Deck does not retain your study content, generated decks, or (in BYOK mode) your API key on its own servers. Open Study Deck uses consent-based Google Analytics to understand aggregate feature usage — it only loads if you allow it, and study content, generated answers, and API keys are never sent as analytics data. Your study decks and preferences stay in your browser's local storage.

How It Works: The BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) Advantage

We do things differently. Instead of locking features behind pricing tiers, Open Study Deck operates on a "Bring Your Own Key" (BYOK) architecture. By hooking up a free Gemini API key directly from Google AI Studio, you unlock a powerful AI text summarizer with no Open Study Deck signup required. Usage is subject to the quota, rate limits, and billing configuration of your own Google AI project — not an Open Study Deck limit.

Here is why this matters: most study platforms upload your files to their own servers, storing and sometimes tracking your study habits behind a usage ceiling. Open Study Deck is a privacy-conscious ai flashcard generator: the interface runs directly inside your web browser, and there is no Open Study Deck account database or deck-storage backend. Your notes, textbook uploads, and generated flashcards stay in your browser's local storage. To generate a card, the text or image you submit is sent directly from your browser to Google Gemini for processing — that is how the AI analysis happens. You never give up an email address or a credit card to use the tool.

Card rendering and PNG export happen locally inside your browser's viewport. By default, your Google Gemini API key is kept only in your browser's session storage for the current tab; if you opt in to "Remember this key on this device," it is saved in your browser's local storage instead so it persists across visits. Either way, the key is used only to authenticate requests you send straight to Google's API endpoints over HTTPS — Open Study Deck has no backend that receives or logs it. There is no Open Study Deck account database or analytics dashboard tracking your study habits.

General Info & Infrastructure

What is Open Study Deck?

Open Study Deck is a privacy-conscious, free visual study card tool designed for high schoolers, university students, and self-learners dealing with massive reading volumes. It runs in your browser and uses the Google Gemini API to transform textbook screenshots, notes, or pasted text into styled active-recall decks. You can try it instantly with Guest Access (no API key, limited daily generations) or connect your own Gemini key with BYOK for additional quota.

How does Guest Access work, and what happens to my content?

Guest Access lets you generate a limited number of study decks per day without configuring anything. Your text or image is sent from your browser to Open Study Deck's own Cloudflare Function, which forwards it to Google Gemini using a shared service credential and returns the result. Open Study Deck does not intentionally retain your submitted content or the generated deck — it is not saved in any Open Study Deck database. A small piece of hashed, non-reversible usage data is kept temporarily just to enforce the daily guest limit; your raw IP address is never stored. Guest generations are protected by Cloudflare Turnstile to prevent automated abuse.

Why is this tool completely free? Where is the catch?

There is no catch. Instead of locking features behind monthly subscription paywalls, Open Study Deck operates on a "Bring Your Own Key" (BYOK) model. We offer this as a fully functional free alternative to AI study apps that gatekeep learning. By linking your own free API key directly from Google AI Studio, you bypass middleman servers and use Gemini directly — subject to whatever quota and rate limits apply to your own Google AI project.

API Key Configuration

How do I get a free Gemini API Key?

Obtaining a key takes under a minute, needs no credit card, and unlocks an ai text summarizer with no Open Study Deck signup required:

  1. Navigate to the official Google AI Studio page.
  2. Sign in with any standard Google account and click the blue "Get API Key" button on the sidebar.
  3. Choose a project, click "Create API Key", and copy your generated secret token.

Paste this code into the Settings tab on Open Study Deck to hook up your workspace interface.

Do I have to re-enter my API key every time I visit the site?

By default, no re-typing is needed within the same browser tab: your key is held in session storage for that tab. If you want it to survive closing the browser and coming back later, check "Remember this key on this device" when you save it — that stores the key in your browser's local storage on this machine instead. Leave that box unchecked on shared or public computers.

Can I use the same API key across my phone and laptop?

Yes. The same Google Gemini API key works across your laptop, tablet, and mobile phone. Because Open Study Deck does not host your credentials in a cloud database, there is no account to sync — you'll need to copy and paste the key into the Settings tab of each individual web browser you use.

When will I be asked to re-enter my API key?

If you haven't checked "Remember this key on this device," your key lives in session storage and clears when you close the browser tab (or the whole browser, depending on your settings) — you'll need to paste it again next visit. If you did check that box, the key persists in local storage until you clear your browser's site data or click "Clear Key" in the Settings panel.

Data Privacy & Document Security

Is my API key safe? Can you see or steal my key?

In BYOK mode, your key never touches an Open Study Deck server — it lives in your browser's session or local storage and is used to query Google's APIs directly over encrypted HTTPS. In Guest Access, there is no personal key at all; requests use a shared credential held only in Open Study Deck's server-side configuration, which the browser never receives. As with any browser-stored secret, keep in mind that browser extensions or scripts running on a compromised device could technically read a BYOK key — only enable "Remember this key" on a device you trust.

Does Open Study Deck use Google Analytics?

Yes, but only with your permission. Open Study Deck uses consent-based Google Analytics to understand aggregate feature usage, such as which study modes are popular and whether generations succeed — this helps us improve the product. The Analytics script is not requested until you click "Allow Analytics" on the consent banner; declining (or ignoring it) means nothing loads and nothing is sent. We never send your study content, generated answers, filenames, deck titles, subject context, or API keys as analytics data. You can change your decision anytime via the "Analytics Preferences" link in the footer.

What happens to the photos of my notes or textbooks?

Textbook screenshots and photos of lecture notes are sent over HTTPS to Google Gemini for analysis — that's how the AI reads and summarizes them. In BYOK mode this goes directly from your browser to Google. In Guest Access it passes through Open Study Deck's Cloudflare Function on the way to Google. In both cases Open Study Deck does not store or cache your images in a database of its own. Once the response comes back, the generated card is rendered and stored only in your browser.
