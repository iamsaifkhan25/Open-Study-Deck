2. Description of Service

Open Study Deck is a multi-page, browser-first, open-source educational study studio. It provides tools for students to summarize notes, generate detailed study outlines, convert screenshots into flashcards, and export formatted revision grids using Google Gemini models. The interface, your library, and your preferences run and stay in your browser; AI generation requests are sent either directly to Google Gemini using your own key (BYOK) or through Open Study Deck's own Cloudflare Function using a shared, rate-limited credential (Guest Access).
