About Us

Our Story & Mission

Open Study Deck was born out of frustration. As students and self-learners, we were exhausted by seeing basic, helpful educational tools locked behind monthly SaaS subscriptions and restrictive analytical paywalls. We believed that study tools shouldn't carry a price tag that gates your grades.

Our mission is to democratize digital learning. By creating a multi-page, serverless environment, we put you in charge of your own data and model allocations. Using Google Gemini API directly from your browser means you get high-fidelity visual cards and textbook summarization completely client-side without subscription popups or tracking.

The BYOK Philosophy

"Bring Your Own Key" (BYOK) is a developer-centric architectural approach that we brought to the student community. When apps host keys, they have to pay for servers, scale infrastructure, and charge you a premium markup. BYOK eliminates the middleman. You grab a free API key from Google AI Studio, input it into your local browser settings, and make queries directly. No signup database, zero cloud hosting costs, and 100% savings passed to you.

Key Studio Capabilities

Every line of code inside the Open Study Deck workshop is tuned for responsive visual execution. Our visual studio allows you to style active meshes, drop textbook screenshots, and output active-recall flashcards as PNG files to your device instantly. It is built to keep up with heavy medical and legal reading volumes without any SaaS limits.
